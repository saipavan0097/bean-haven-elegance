# Phase 2: Premium Brand Enhancements

## Goal
Enhance the existing Bean Haven experience without changing its bronze coffee identity, typography, navigation, routing, or overall section structure.

## What will change
- Refine the hero’s spacing, text hierarchy, image presentation, CTA motion, and subtle scroll parallax.
- Upgrade the existing editorial menu cards with ratings, Quick View, Order Now, and premium interaction states.
- Add a Featured Coffee section using six existing drinks and the current visual language.
- Turn Our Story into a richer editorial composition with a concise brand timeline.
- Update Why Choose Us content and add refined icon motion while retaining its six-card structure.
- Convert the gallery into a responsive masonry-style arrangement while preserving its lightbox.
- Polish the reservation form and provide an on-page confirmation state after submission.
- Refine testimonial cards and carousel controls with the existing glass treatment.
- Update the newsletter offer to include a free-coffee coupon after signup.
- Preserve the contact details, social links, business hours, and map placeholder while improving polish only where needed.
- Add a brief coffee-bean loading transition on first page load.
- Add an elegant footer credit: “Designed & Developed by Sai Pavan Rali” with GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, email, and WhatsApp links supplied by the user.

## Technical details
- Reuse current images, semantic color tokens, typography, Reveal animations, buttons, and data patterns.
- Keep images lazy-loaded outside the hero and preserve accessible labels, keyboard behavior, and responsive layouts.
- Add only small reusable components needed for Featured Coffee, quick view, loading, and confirmation states.
- Verify desktop and mobile rendering, interactions, overflow, console output, and TypeScript correctness.

## Scope guard
No redesign of the brand, navbar, routing, color palette, typography system, or removal of existing sections.
